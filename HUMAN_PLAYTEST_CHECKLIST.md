Use a fresh isolated lab session. Let the player try the first minute without coaching, unless they ask for help. Record what they do and their words separately from the facilitator's interpretation. Do not require Perfect Dodge or an unassisted English response to progress.

## First attempt: combat and clarity

- [ ] Can the player start, identify movement/attack/dodge, and explain the current goal?
- [ ] Do attacks feel connected to pressing J or tapping the attack control? Record missed or apparently ignored inputs.
- [ ] Can the player discover or repeat the three-hit combo? Record attempts, highest combo and any timing confusion.
- [ ] Does the third strike feel stronger than the first two? Ask why; record the participant's words.
- [ ] Do movement acceleration, stopping and direction changes feel controllable?
- [ ] Can the player identify a slime warning before the attack lands?
- [ ] Does dodge travel where expected? Record accidental dodges toward danger.
- [ ] Is Perfect Dodge understandable when it happens? Was it recognized without explanation?
- [ ] Can the player distinguish heavy jump, rush and crystal-related boss attacks?
- [ ] Is phase II noticeable without feeling unfair? Record deaths and the perceived cause.
- [ ] Can the player recognize an exposed core and use the English instruction to exploit it?
- [ ] Do runes, projectile, collision, explosion and damage appear connected during magic?
- [ ] Are damage numbers and screen shake readable? Try Off/Low/Normal and reduced motion.
- [ ] Do any particles, overlays or touch controls hide an enemy, warning or essential action?
- [ ] Can the player pause, resume, restart after defeat and return to the menu without assistance?

## English interaction

- [ ] With authorized recordings installed, can the player understand each instruction before viewing text? Record the exact voice ID and replay count.
- [ ] Is pronunciation, stress, rhythm, speed and character consistency natural and intelligible? **Pending until real recordings can be heard.**
- [ ] Is normal playback comfortable? Does slow playback remain intelligible without sounding distorted?
- [ ] Can the player find replay, English subtitles and Korean hints?
- [ ] Are voices clear over music/effects? Compare levels before, during and after speech.
- [ ] Do wrong answers explain the expression without feeling punitive?
- [ ] Does the player understand how English comprehension changes the battle?
- [ ] Do pauses for listening and repeated sentence magic happen too often? Record where the player expresses impatience.
- [ ] With audio muted or unavailable, can the player finish using text and Korean help?
- [ ] Does the result clearly distinguish text-assisted work from actual independent listening?
- [ ] Can the player find expressions to review and explain one phrase in their own words after the fight?

## Motivation and optional replay

- [ ] Ask whether the first minute was engaging and what made it so or not.
- [ ] Ask what felt repetitive, unclear or slow. Do not suggest a preferred answer.
- [ ] Offer the replay option without urging the player to take it; record the choice.
- [ ] If replayed, ask what they want to improve: combo, damage received, dodge, listening or another goal.
- [ ] Does the result summary help choose that goal without overwhelming the player?
- [ ] Does the player want a further encounter? Record the reason, not only yes/no.
