Warm, calm, patient, wise; clear conversational General American.

Energetic, brief and expressive; clear stress without shouting.

Soft, mysterious and highly intelligible; optional subtle reverberation only after intelligibility review.

## Production and acceptance

Use legally redistributable proficient-speaker recordings or clearly identified high-quality synthetic speech; never imitate a real identifiable person. One clean utterance per normal-speed file, no music, comfortable natural rhythm and complete consonants. Record an optional genuinely slower take only if useful; otherwise the player uses pitch-preserving 0.75x playback. Do not call time-stretched playback a separately recorded slow take.

Record duration, source, model/performer, commercial redistribution rights and quality review in the manifest. The build embeds only actual local files with license/source metadata. Review every English/Korean pair, pronunciation, stress, consistent speaker identity, loudness, clipping and both speed modes through real listening. Native audio events alone cannot certify those qualities. All 34 phrases are reachable from expression practice; the core six taught expressions are attached to gameplay.
